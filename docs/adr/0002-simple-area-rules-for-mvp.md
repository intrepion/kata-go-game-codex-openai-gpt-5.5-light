# Simple Area Rules for MVP

The MVP will use simple area scoring rather than Japanese-style territory scoring, Chinese-style rule variants, or selectable rulesets. This gives the first playable version a clear, testable endgame model while leaving more formal ruleset variants for later work.

## Considered Options

- Japanese-style territory scoring
- Chinese-style area scoring
- Simple area scoring
- User-selectable rulesets

## Consequences

The first implementation should prioritize legal move handling, captures, ko prevention, pass flow, and area scoring clarity. Ruleset-specific disputes and advanced adjudication are out of scope until the core local game is trustworthy.
