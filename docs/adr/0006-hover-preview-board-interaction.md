# Hover Preview Board Interaction

Players will place stones by hovering or focusing an intersection to preview the move, then clicking or tapping to confirm it. This keeps 19x19 play precise without adding a separate confirmation step to every move.

## Considered Options

- Click or tap exact intersections only
- Hover preview with click or tap confirmation
- Click or tap intersection, then confirm button
- Drag stones from a bowl onto the board

## Consequences

The board interface must make the target intersection visible before placement, and touch play must provide an equivalent preview-or-clear-target behavior where hover is unavailable.
